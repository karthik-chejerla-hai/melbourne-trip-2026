        Good to know

        • Puffing Billy runs daily except Christmas Day. On Total Fire Ban days it may switch to diesel or change timetables, and it closes on Catastrophic fire danger days. 28 Dec is the swap day.
        • Boxing Day Test: Australia v New Zealand at the MCG, 26 to 30 December, play from 10:30am. MCG tours are unlikely to run on Test days, so the plan takes in Day 3 on Mon 28 Dec.
        • Penguin Parade: penguins arrive around 8:30pm in December and you leave close to 10pm. Plan a toddler nap and pack warm layers, even in summer. Closed 25 December.
        • Lune: four Melbourne shops. Maxi Lune on Lonsdale St stays open until 5pm and is the easiest bet. Holiday hours change each year.
        • Fire danger: check the Hazards Near Me (NSW) and VicEmergency apps before each coast and day-trip drive.
